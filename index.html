About

Full-stack curiosity, end-to-end ownership

I like the whole stack of a problem — not just the model or the code, but the infrastructure underneath it, the compliance paperwork around it, and the person on the other end using it. As a founding CTO, I've had to own all of it: designing AI pipelines, deploying them on cloud infrastructure, getting them through enterprise security review, and refining the product until it earns its users.

My background is Mathematics–Computer Science at UC San Diego (3.9 GPA, Provost's Honors), with stops in ML engineering and AI consulting along the way. The thread through everything: technology should widen who gets to participate — whether that's video for people who can't see it, or coding classes for kids who'd never been offered one.

I grew up between worlds — family in Singapore and India, education and career in California — and I think it shows in how I work: comfortable switching contexts, allergic to assuming everyone shares my defaults, and happiest when something I built travels further than I expected.

Off the clock I cook (genuinely, not aspirationally), build side projects, and protect a Wordle streak with unreasonable seriousness.
